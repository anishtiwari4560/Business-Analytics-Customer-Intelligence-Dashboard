# Business Analytics & Customer Intelligence Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end business analytics dashboard focused on understanding sales performance, profitability, product performance, returns, and customer value.

The dashboard transforms transactional business data into decision-oriented insights across four analytical areas:

1. **Monthly Business Performance**
2. **Sales & Profitability Analysis**
3. **Product Performance & Profitability**
4. **Customer Analytics & Customer Value**

The objective is not only to report KPIs, but also to identify performance changes, high- and low-performing products, return patterns, and customer segments that can support business decisions.

---

## 🎯 Key Business Questions

- How are sales, profit, orders, customers, and returns performing?
- How have sales and profit changed over time?
- Which product sub-categories generate the most sales and profit?
- Which products have stronger or weaker profit margins?
- Which product categories have higher return rates?
- What is the value and purchasing behavior of different customer segments?
- Which customers have the highest customer lifetime value (CLV)?
- Which customer groups may require retention attention?

---

## 📈 Dashboard Highlights

### 1. Monthly Business Performance

The executive dashboard tracks:

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Active Customers
- Return Rate
- Sales & Profit Trends
- Sales by Category
- Sales by Customer Segment
- Top Sub-Categories
- Orders by Day
- Return Orders and Return Rate

The dashboard also provides an executive recommendation based on the observed decline in sales, profit, and orders.

### 2. Sales & Profitability Analysis

This section examines longer-term business performance through:

- Average Order Value
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin
- Profit Margin Over Time
- Profit vs. Sales Variance
- Sales Growth
- Profit Growth

### 3. Product Performance & Profitability

Product-level analysis includes:

- Sales vs. Profitability Matrix
- Profit Margin by Sub-Category
- Total Profit by Sub-Category
- Return Rate by Sub-Category
- Top and bottom performing product groups

### 4. Customer Analytics & Customer Value

Customer intelligence includes:

- Customer Count
- Average Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
- Customer Revenue
- Purchase Frequency
- Repeat Rate
- Customer Return Rate
- Customer Value Matrix
- RFM Segmentation
- Customer CLV Distribution
- Top Customers by CLV
- Customer Revenue vs. Profit

## 🔍 Selected Insights

The dashboard identifies several important business signals:

- Sales: **-23.39%**
- Profit: **-29.46%**
- Orders: **-17.65%**
- Return Rate: **7.54%**
- Total historical sales: approximately **$7.94M**
- Total historical profit: approximately **$934K**
- Profit margin: approximately **11.76%**
- Average Order Value: approximately **$246.60**
- Average Customer CLV: approximately **$25.77K**
- Repeat Rate: approximately **99.37%**

The customer analysis also uses RFM segmentation to distinguish groups such as Champions, Loyal Customers, At Risk, Lost Customers, Big Spenders, New Customers, and Potential Customers.

## 💡 Business Value

The project demonstrates how business data can be converted into actionable intelligence by combining:

**Descriptive Analytics → Diagnostic Analysis → Customer Segmentation → Business Recommendations**

Rather than focusing only on visual reporting, the dashboard connects performance metrics to potential business actions, particularly around declining profitability, product performance, returns, and customer retention.
